The CPT Combat Mobility Routine

Welcome to the CPT community! Here is your daily 15-minute fight mobility routine.

Phase 1 · 4 Minutes

Hip & Pelvis Decompression

90/90 Hip Flow: 2 minutes per side. Rotate smoothly between internal and external rotation to restore deep hip socket clearance for high kicks and guard recovery.

Cossack Squats: 3 sets of 8 reps per side. Keep trailing heel grounded to stretch the adductors and build knee resiliency.

Phase 2 · 4 Minutes

Thoracic Spine & Reach

Half-Split Thoracic Rotation: 3 sets of 10 reps per side. Unlocks upper back rotation, allowing you to throw punches with full extension without straining your lower back.

Floor Angels: 2 minutes continuous slow glide against the mat to reset tight chest muscles.

Phase 3 · 4 Minutes

Ankles & Sprawl Agility

Knee-to-Wall Dorsiflexion: 15 pulses per side. Essential for explosive spring on the balls of your feet and absorbing leg kicks.

Deep Squat Hold with Reach: 2 minutes total time. Sink into the bottom position, chest up, alternating overhead reaches.

Phase 4 · 3 Minutes

Recovery & Parasympathetic Reset

Box Breathing (4-4-4-4): 3 minutes diaphragmatic breathing on your back with legs elevated.

Anchialo Magnesium Gel Application: Massage natural magnesium gel into tight calves, shoulders, and lower back to calm the central nervous system.
